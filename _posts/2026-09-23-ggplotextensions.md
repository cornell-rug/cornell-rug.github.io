On Wednesday, September 23 at 4:00pm, we'll meet in Mann 102 and on Zoom ([link](https://cornell.zoom.us/j/95723147511?pwd=yTARsRFrUPSD3SaHdUAeJiab6obDZI.1)) for our next R User Group meeting.

The ggplot2 package provides many tools for making graphs and visualizations, and the R community has created additional packages to build on ggplot. In this session, I’ll highlight some of the interesting packages that extend the ggplot foundation, a few places to sort through the packages, and some ways of thinking about the different ways that packages extend ggplot.
